What counts as slop?

The index read three planes of a paper's reasoning. SciSlop v2 names seventeen ways it breaks, in five areas: claims without evidence, parts that do not connect, choices never explained, words that carry nothing, and rhetoric in place of evidence. Judged the same way whoever wrote the paper.
