Reads the paper, not the prose.

AI text detectors score sentences. The Science Slop Index counts, for six patterns, the share of a paper's units in which the scientific reasoning fails to connect. Each pattern is more common in AI-generated papers than in human-written papers on the same problem.

Structure

Do the sections build on one another?

  • Cross-section referencesFigures, tables, and sections that no other section points to.
  • Macro redundancyLater sections that restate earlier ones instead of developing them.
Argument

Are claims and citations argued, not just stated?

  • Argument graphKey claims stated before anything leads up to them.
  • Citation isolationPrior work cited without being related to any other work.
Artifacts

Can a reader inspect the method and the evidence?

  • Figure expositionMethod figures crowded with detail that belongs in the text.
  • Evidence gapResult tables with no concrete input, output, or case anywhere.

One number, three planes

The index averages the measures within each plane, then averages the planes. On SciSlopBench, 390 AI-generated papers each paired with a human-written paper on the same problem, it identifies the AI-generated paper in 85.9% of pairs; Binoculars, the strongest text detector, reaches 68.7%.

How it works

Six measures, three planes, one number

Each measure counts the share of a paper's units that show one pattern. The index averages the measures within each plane, then averages the planes, and reports the result out of 100. Higher means more of the patterns that set AI-generated papers apart from matched human papers.

What happens to a paper

Every report receives a key that reopens it later. Uploaded files are deleted after analysis; only the report and rendered figures stay.

1Read

LaTeX source is read directly; arXiv links fetch the source and the PDF. PDFs are rebuilt into sections, captions, references, and citations.

2Measure

Four measures are exact counting rules. Argument graph and Figure exposition ask a language model to label sentences and read the method figure.

3Highlight

Every flagged unit is placed back on the PDF, colored by plane, with a graph per measure. Export as highlighted PDF, JSON, or CSV.

Where this site differs from the paper

  • Argument graph. The paper picks each claim's supporting sentence by pointwise mutual information under a second model. The model used here returns no log-probabilities, so it chooses the sentence the claim rests on from the other Introduction sentences, shown in shuffled order so position cannot bias the choice.
  • Citation isolation. The paper's frozen list of relational cues is not public; this site uses a reconstructed list.
  • Figure exposition. The method figure is found by a caption gate (overview, pipeline, framework, architecture, workflow, schematic). When none passes, you can choose the figure in the report.
  • Pair accuracy shown above is the paper's result for its own implementation; this site re-implements the measures.
