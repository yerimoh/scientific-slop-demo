Paper-level scientific slop

Science, or slop?

AI text detectors read sentences. The Science Slop Index reads how a paper holds together: whether its sections build on each other, its claims are argued, and its evidence can be inspected.

How it works

Six measures, three planes, one number

Each measure counts the share of a paper's units that show one pattern. The index averages the measures within each plane, then averages the planes, and reports the result out of 100. Higher means more of the patterns that set AI-generated papers apart from matched human papers.

* Pair accuracy is the paper's reported result for its own implementation on SciSlopBench. This site re-implements the measures; the differences are listed below.

It separates AI from human papers

As reported in the paper: pair accuracy on SciSlopBench, 390 AI-generated papers each matched with a human paper on the same problem. 0.5 is chance.

What happens to your paper

Every report gets a key. Keep it to open the report later.

  1. Read. LaTeX source is read directly; arXiv links fetch the source and the PDF. PDFs are rebuilt into sections, captions, references, and citations.
  2. Measure. Four measures are exact counting rules. Argument graph and Figure exposition ask a language model to label sentences and read the method figure.
  3. Highlight. Every flagged unit is placed back on the PDF, colored by plane, and can be downloaded as a highlighted PDF.

One number

S(p) = mean over planes of ( mean over the plane's applicable measures of sj(p) ), and the index is 100 · S(p). A measure that does not apply to a paper is skipped, not counted as zero.

Where this site differs from the paper

  • Argument graph. The paper picks each claim's supporting sentence by pointwise mutual information under a second model. The model used here returns no log-probabilities, so it chooses the sentence the claim rests on from the other Introduction sentences, shown in shuffled order so position cannot bias the choice.
  • Citation isolation. The paper's frozen list of relational cues is not public; this site uses a reconstructed list.
  • Figure exposition. The method figure is found by a caption gate (overview, pipeline, framework, architecture, workflow, schematic). When none passes, you can choose the figure in the report.
  • Index bands. Low, Moderate, High, and Very high are descriptive cut-points on the share of flagged units, not a probability of AI authorship.
